# About Junteng Liu

I am a first-year PhD candidate in **Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, advised by **Prof. Junxian He** in the **HKUST NLP Group**. My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**, with a particular interest in **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**, **hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)**, and **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science** (2024–present) — Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Junxian He (HKUST NLP Group).
- **B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering** (2020–2024) — Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduated June 2024.
- **Honors:** Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

## Research Experience

- **Research Intern at MINIMAX** — February 2025–present.
- **Research Intern at Tencent WXG** — June 2024–September 2024, advised by **Zifei Shan**.
- **Research Intern at Shanghai AI Lab** — June 2023–December 2023, advised by **Prof. Yu Cheng**.

## Skills

- **Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML)** — Large language models (LLMs), self-supervised learning, model analysis.
- **LLM Reasoning & Reinforcement Learning** — Chain-of-thought reasoning, reinforcement learning.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)** — Perception bottleneck and hallucination in vision-language models.
- **LLM Truthfulness & Interpretability** — Calibration, truthfulness, and interpretability of large language models.
